Seven rooftops stand above a sea of fog, and the fog is fatal. Swing between them with the grappling hook on your left hand. You start with a dagger, and a sword, spear or crossbow floats above some of the roofs. Ninja knives, a boomerang, a longbow, a cannon and a jetpack are in the shop. Twenty red soldiers guard the roofs, and hunters swing after you from roof to roof. Cut them down or knock them into the fog, and spend the coins you earn on stronger weapons in the shop. Online, you can fight your friends or team up with them.
