Seven rooftops stand above a sea of fog, and the fog is fatal. Swing between them with the grappling hook on your left hand. You start with a dagger, and better weapons float above some of the roofs: grab a sword, spear, crossbow, ninja knives, a cannon or a jetpack. Twenty red soldiers guard the roofs, and hunters swing after you from roof to roof. Cut them down or knock them into the fog. Online, you can fight your friends or team up with them.
